MANUAL

t-rec is a toolbox. Each tool does one thing and lives
in its own folder. No account, no server, no tracking.

  notes       Plain-text notes. Saves while you type.
              Two layouts: one note at a time, or all
              of them at once as cards. Export the lot
              to a single .md file.

  bookmarks   Links as cover cards. A card holds an
              image, a title, a progress line, a URL.

Every tool starts empty and keeps its data in this
browser only. Nothing follows you to another browser,
another device, or another address.
